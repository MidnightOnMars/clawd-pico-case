# RESIN-V1: fork notes (Formlabs SLA variant)

This fork carries a variant of this case for Formlabs SLA resin
printers. Everything on `main` not listed below is upstream; only the files below
differ.

## What changed vs upstream `stl/current` (v1.9+J24)

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `cad/v1_7_lock.py` | `HOOK` 0.5 -> 0.45 mm; closing ramp `Z_RAMP_TOP` +2.4 -> +3.0 (28 deg -> 18.8 deg from vertical); lid catch pocket 0.55 -> 0.50 deep, skin 0.85 -> 0.90 |
| `cad/resin_v1_assemble.py` | new: assembles the resin set, uses the J24-B full-flange joystick, and copies upstream's J24-A in beside it |
| `stl/resin-v1/` | lid, base, joystick (J24-B: full 0.40 mm flange, was 0.24 mm thin edge), joystick-j24-a (J24-A, byte-identical to upstream's tested cap), button (byte-identical to tested v1.0), full-set, `resin-v1.json` (hashes) |
| `stl/resin-v1/README.md` | print sheet: first-run print list, what each part needs, wash/cure, first-fit checklist |

Nothing else changed: the assembled case is still 33.04 x 58.6 x 25.64 mm (18.67 mm
is the height of the base part alone). `button.stl` and `joystick-j24-a.stl` are
byte-identical to upstream's tested exports. The other parts are
regenerated by the modified CAD: the lid is upstream's v1.9 lid and the base is
upstream's v1.7 base (the same pairing Austin's tested `stl/current/` uses; see
`sources` in `stl/current/current.json`), both re-exported with the eased snap
constants. `joystick.stl` is J24-B (full 0.40 mm flange); upstream's J24-A
thin-edge cap sits beside it as `joystick-j24-a.stl`.

## Validation

Repo checks all pass (6 catches, single solids, joystick caught-on-pull with
5.1 deg minimum tilt clearance, <=45 deg overhangs, hardware clearances).
Independent mesh measurement: joystick min thickness 0.400 mm (was 0.240),
lid min wall 0.89 mm (was 0.85), all parts watertight.

Second mesh check, 2026-11-12, on the committed STLs and without the CAD: every part
is a watertight single solid and matches its hash in `resin-v1.json`. Against
upstream's tested `stl/current` parts, the lid and base differ only at the six
catches and pockets, and the joystick only in the flange. Assembled, the resin lid
and base behave like the tested pair: they touch only at the twelve crush ribs, the
catches engage after 0.04 mm of lift, and the side gap is 0.20 mm. The CAD was not
re-run for this check.

## Why these changes

Resin SLA parts are stiffer and more notch-sensitive than PETG FDM parts, and
they are dimensionally more accurate. The thin 0.24 mm joystick flange was the
weakest feature (at the limit of Formlabs' 0.2 mm minimum wall thickness guidance
and load-bearing); the
snap was eased because the clear resin lid skirt is the flexing member and
resin does not yield like PETG.

J24-B is upstream's own variant, and upstream passed it over. Austin printed A and
B in PETG on 2026-10-02 and chose A: "B clicks the buttons a little bit too clunky. A is the one"
(`prints/2026-10-02-joystick-j24.md`). In the model B's flange meets the lid
underside at 5.1 to 8.2 degrees of tilt against 6.9 to 10.4 for A, and the stick
tilts about 8, so the clunk is geometry and should carry over to resin. Both caps
are in `stl/resin-v1/` so the first run can settle it.

## What was not changed, and should be watched

Found in review on 2026-11-12, before the first print. Every fit below was tuned by
trial on an FDM printer in PETG and is unchanged in this variant. On an SLA printer
each can come out different. The print sheet's first-run list and First fit steps
cover them.

| Fit | Designed value | Source | Risk in resin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lid crush ribs | 12 ribs, 0.15 mm into the base tongue | `cad/v1_4_rounded.py` (V1.4-RIB) | PETG ribs give; resin ribs may make a hard press fit and leave the clear skirt under load |
| Joystick socket | 1.90 mm square on a 1.86 mm stem | `cad/joystick_j24_fit.py`, MEASUREMENTS J4 | FDM holes print tight; an accurate socket may sit loose |
| Catch free play | 0.04 mm | `cad/s2_tight_base.py` (S2-CATCH) | less than one 0.1 mm layer |
| LCD board capture | lid pads 0.05 mm above the board; 0.14 mm end play | `cad/params.py` (LID_PAD_GAP), MEASUREMENTS V1.3-L1-RECHECK | rigid parts can pinch the board |
| USB opening | 0.10 mm under the connector | MEASUREMENTS V1-USB | connector or plug may foul |
| Button retention | lips 0.075 and 0.20 mm | `cad/params.py` (CAP_FLANGE_W, CAP_FLANGE_D) | fine for a rigid cap; a rubber cap may pull out |

The eased snap also runs against upstream's latest finding: on 2026-11-10 Austin
noted that the PETG cases get easier to open with use and began testing a base with
a 0.54 mm hook. If the resin case opens too easily, put `HOOK` back to 0.5 and keep
the longer ramp.

The export orientation is inherited from FDM too, and for one part it must not be
used as the print orientation. The joystick cap is exported flange down. Formlabs
says PreForm merges the first 0.75 mm of a part printed directly on the build
platform into one combined shape. On this cap that zone holds the whole flange and
the socket mouth: merged, the 0.40 mm flange (0.24 mm at the rim on J24-A) is a
0.75 mm slab on both caps, and the 3.10 mm mouth, taken as a union of the solid,
closes to about 2.3 mm against a 2.94 mm collar. Computed from the meshes on
2026-11-12, not test-printed. The lid loses 0.11 mm over the button pocket and
0.05 mm over the joystick pocket the same way; the base and the buttons are
unaffected. The print sheet carries the table.

## Rebuild

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/bin/python cad/v1_7_lock.py   # base + lid, validated
.venv/bin/python cad/v1_9.py        # v1.9 lid + joystick reference
.venv/bin/python cad/joystick_j24.py # A and B joysticks, validated
.venv/bin/python cad/resin_v1_assemble.py  # writes stl/resin-v1/
```

Two cautions. The resin constants are edited in place in `cad/v1_7_lock.py`, so this
fork's CAD no longer rebuilds upstream's PETG lid and base; `stl/current/` holds
Austin's tested files as committed and should not be regenerated here. And the
first two scripts overwrite `stl/v1.7`, `stl/v1.9` and `renders/` with resin-constant
output: `resin_v1_assemble.py` copies the lid and base from there, so run all four in
order, then restore those folders with `git checkout -- stl/v1.7 stl/v1.9 renders`
and do not commit them. The `sources` paths in `resin-v1.json` name those
intermediate files, not the committed PETG ones.

## Printing

See `stl/resin-v1/README.md`. One Form Studio 1: Alpha Clear lid and base, Tough
2000 V2 joystick (print both `joystick-j24-a.stl` and `joystick.stl`), Flexible 80A
V2 buttons (x4). All three resins are Form Studio 1
compatible. The STLs are printer-agnostic: on another Formlabs SLA printer, use a
resin that machine supports (Alpha Clear is Form Studio 1 only; Clear Resin V5 on
a Form 4 series). `full-set.stl` is all seven parts on one plate in one colour:
print either it or the individual parts, not both. Do not print the joystick caps,
or the `full-set.stl` plate, flange down directly on the build platform.
